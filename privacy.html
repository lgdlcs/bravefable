Privacy policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Bravefable makes personalized picture books from a photo of your child. We know that's a lot of trust. This policy explains, in plain words, what we collect, why, and what we never do.

1. What we collect

  • Waitlist: your email address, your child's age range and the story you're interested in.
  • Orders (once we launch): one photo of your child, your child's first name, the details you choose to include in the story, and your shipping and contact details. Payments are handled by our payment provider; we never see or store your full card number.

2. Your child's photo

  • We use the photo once, to draw your child's illustrated character.
  • The photo is deleted as soon as you approve the character. Pages of the book are drawn from the illustrated character, not from the photo.
  • Your child's photo and character are never used to train AI models, ours or anyone else's.
  • We use no-storage and no-retention settings with the AI providers that help us draw, and we send the photo only once.
  • We never sell, share or publish your child's photo, name or book.
  • Live preview: the photo is sent once to our drawing provider with no-storage settings and is never stored by us. The preview images are shown once and deleted within 10 minutes. We keep your email and the story you chose to follow up, until launch or until you ask us to delete them.

3. Why we use your data

To manage your reservation and keep you informed about the launch, to make, print and ship your book (orders), and to answer your questions. We only process what's necessary for these purposes, based on your consent and on the performance of our contract with you.

4. Who we share it with

Only the service providers we need to run Bravefable: email delivery, AI image generation (under the settings above), printing and shipping, and payment (Stripe, which handles your card details; we never see or store them). Each one is bound by a data processing agreement and may only use your data to provide its service to us.

5. How long we keep it

  • Child's photo: deleted once you approve the character.
  • Illustrated character and book files: kept for 90 days after delivery so we can reprint a damaged copy, then deleted, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Reservation details (email, child's age range, story chosen): until launch plus the time needed for your order, or until you ask us to delete them. Payment records are kept as long as tax law requires.

6. Children's privacy

Our books are for children, but our service is for parents and guardians. Only a parent or legal guardian may upload a child's photo or place an order. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your data and your child's, and to stop sending you emails. Residents of California and of the European Union and United Kingdom have these rights by law (CCPA/CPRA, GDPR). Write to hello@bravefable.com and we'll answer within 30 days.

8. Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies. If we add privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics, we'll update this page.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@bravefable.com.
